Overview

What is Pervasive Insights™?

Pervasive Insights™ is the AI suite of City Research Solutions, a full-service consumer research firm in the Madison, Wisconsin area, established in 1979. It turns a brand's existing research history into a private insights portal: past studies become searchable in plain English, and a synthetic panel calibrated to how that brand's real customers actually answered lets teams survey, interview, and run group research on demand in about 90 seconds.

What does Pervasive Insights™ do?

Pervasive Insights™ indexes the research a brand has already paid for and turns it into a working portal. Three things come out of it:

  • A searchable research library. Every past study becomes searchable in plain English, so anyone on the team can find an answer in seconds.
  • Synthesis across studies. Data is searchable across projects, so you can spot a trend that no single study was designed to show.
  • An on-demand synthetic panel. A panel of your buyers, calibrated to your real data, that you can survey, interview, or run as a focus group, with answers in about 90 seconds.

Who is it for?

Brands and agencies of almost any size, from Fortune 100 companies to regional players and high-tech startups. You do not need a large research history to start:

  • A few past studies. Even a handful of prior studies can calibrate a useful set of topics, and we typically use all of it.
  • A large corpus. If you have hundreds of studies, you choose how much to bring in, from a recent slice to your entire history.
  • Little or no prior research yet. We calibrate from scratch with a couple of targeted surveys, reaching a working panel within weeks of signing.

It is especially valuable for teams that field research only once or twice a year, when most questions otherwise never get asked because fielding is too slow or too expensive.

What makes it different?

  • Your data, not generic data. The panel is calibrated to how your customers actually answered, so it mirrors your buyers rather than an average internet user.
  • Senior-only teams. Every engagement is run by senior insights professionals with decades of experience, with no junior hand-offs.
  • Deep research heritage. The methods draw on five decades of commissioned research and hundreds of thousands of human respondents.
  • Honest about trust. Every answer carries a visible calibration status, so you always know how much weight to put on it.

Frequently asked questions

Is the synthetic panel generic or custom?

It is custom. The panel is calibrated to your own prior research, so it reflects how your customers answer rather than an average internet user.

How fast are answers?

About 90 seconds for a typical survey readout.

Does it replace human research?

No. It complements it. You front-load cheap, fast iteration on the synthetic panel, then validate high-stakes decisions with human respondents.
